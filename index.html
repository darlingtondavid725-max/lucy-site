LUCY

Link your WhatsApp to Lucy

Enter your number to get a pairing code.

How to use the code: open WhatsApp, go to Settings, then Linked devices, then Link a device, then "Link with phone number instead", and type the code.

By linking, your number will follow Lucy's two WhatsApp channels. You can unfollow them any time.

This is an unofficial bot. WhatsApp can limit or ban numbers that use bots, so use a number you can afford to risk. You can unlink at any time in Linked devices.
